Oval Runner placement study

Decision: Advance one candidate to an official practice run. The only competition result measured here was V5 at 4.08, on commit 0eb6677. Every placement result below comes from the proxy. The official team page supplied the total across 48 games. Its submission history and watch page exposed no V5 per-match death traces.

Baseline deaths

The kit contains the protocol, wrappers, map, checker and a 300-observation example stream. That example ends in 13th place and belongs to the checker. The documented observation includes a living-player count and five seconds of continued tracking after a mech leaves view. The existing duel and opening harnesses already contain opponents, while neither produces a 16-player placement.

Across the initial 128 proxy matches, V5 was last alive 8 times and died 120 times. On the fatal interval, 117 deaths involved encounter HP loss alone, two involved both an encounter and the storm, and one involved the storm alone. Median death time was 84 proxy seconds. The last mode was recovery in 63 deaths and zone movement in 57. Only eight deaths included a duel in the final ten seconds. At least four mode switches appeared in 82 death traces.

Median HP by time before death was 1,181 at ten seconds (112 traces long enough), 775 at five seconds (120), 94 at one second (120), and zero at death. The trace file preserves each timestamp, HP and mode. For example, focused-field seed 12000 died at 14 seconds, after falling from 1,066 HP at 4 seconds to 198 HP at 13 seconds. It switched from evasion to recovery at 12 seconds.

What changes in the submission

During an active shrink, a healthy bot with a visible mech can continue its existing activity when the estimated HP cost of returning to the moving boundary falls below 3% of current HP. The test uses a 1.5-second boundary forecast and walking speed, requires at least 65% HP and more than two seconds of estimated time to spare, and excludes retreat and a zero-radius destination. The allowance estimates a return cost on each observation. Cumulative storm damage can exceed 3%.

Set OVAL_STORM_ALLOWANCE=0 to disable the feature. Off matched V5's placement, cause, end time, HP and mode history on all 128 original training matches. The final single-feature build matched the screened build's full result and history in all 512 three-stage held-out matches. The rejected switches are preserved in placement-experiments.patch against V5 for reproducibility.

Regression and latency checks

Separate V5 and candidate images loaded their own Rust libraries and ran the same fixtures. Every case-level outcome matched across 928 cases. The race tests covered 64 storm routes (mean 58.125 HP damage, 266 total cubes), 64 supply races (62 captures), 64 distant-observer openings (64 first cubes), and 64 nearby-observer openings (64 original responses). The 96 economy cases averaged 8.51 cubes, with 87 gear pickups and 51 supplies. The 576 repair cases yielded 336 survivors, with identical terminal HP, cubes and time for every pair.

The candidate passed 16 policy scenarios and seven pressure/capture checks, reached 256/256 navigation goals and re-entered 64/64 circles. It won 48/48 simplified starter duels. In the official checker both images answered 300/300 observations within 50 ms. V5 startup/p99/max was 597/2.5/5.4 ms. The candidate was 592/2.4/5.0 ms. These are local Docker measurements.

What the proxy measures

The 16 agents share a synthetic flat disk with four chasers, four campers, four zone-followers and three looters. All contestants can gain cube HP and share the same proximity-based random HP exchange, independent of the entrant's chosen mode. The independent field assigns exchanges to the nearest contestant. In the focused field, nearby rivals favor the entrant. The bot receives game-shaped observations and runs its built controller. Competing runs share spawns, crate-free cube locations, opponent roles and per-slot/per-second random draws even after their trajectories diverge. Same-tick deaths receive equal points based on earlier eliminations. Clock survivors are ranked by HP.

Matches last up to 120 proxy seconds. The 10 Hz sensitivity run advances the entrant and its ring observations between one-second opponent and encounter updates. Flat visibility within range and five seconds of tracking replace occlusion. There are no weapon trajectories, cover, directional audio, crates, upgrade offers, gear or supply captures in this placement model. A separate harness exercises many of those resource decisions. Genuine practice opponents, full-match dynamics, simultaneous-death tie details and regional map behavior can differ. The proxy baseline itself scores far above 4.08, so absolute scores and the size of any official gain are uncalibrated.

Unseen-attacker bearing responses, the unseen-enemy grid and its downstream duel/loot/planner/roam/final-ring/evade decisions, and cover positioning were left unmeasured. This proxy has no observation process to validate those estimates. No performance claim is made for them. A four-value sensitivity sweep around 3% was sufficient to choose a threshold for this round.

Submission order

  1. Keep V5 at 4.08 as the scored reference. Use the exact single-feature branch commit for one official practice submission. If the scorer accepts only a repository default branch, promote that exact tree to the accepted ref when ready.
  2. On a tie, retain the earlier V5 submission. On a lower score, also retain V5. On an increase, use the new result as the reference. One 48-match score provides no per-match uncertainty estimate.
  3. Hold the duel discount, zone hold and equal-performing variants out of the submission queue. A second practice run should test a separately validated hypothesis.

Reproducing the measurements

Build the baseline and candidate in separate checkouts. Set KIT to the kit, STUDY to the study checkout, and RESULTS to a writable directory. Run the same seed range for each tag. The placement_summary.py command pairs by field and seed. Use --ring staged --decisions 10 for the faster cadence test.

docker build --platform linux/amd64 -t oval-runner:v5 /path/to/v5
docker build --platform linux/amd64 -t oval-runner:candidate "$STUDY"
for tag in v5 candidate; do
  docker run --rm --platform linux/amd64 --network none --read-only --cpus 1 --memory 512m \
    -v "$KIT:/kit:ro" -v "$RESULTS:/results" -v "$STUDY/evaluate.py:/bot/evaluate.py:ro" \
    -v "$STUDY/placement.py:/bot/placement.py:ro" --entrypoint python "oval-runner:$tag" \
    -Bu placement.py --kit /kit --start 47000 --count 256 --ring staged --output "/results/$tag.jsonl"
done
python3 placement_summary.py "$RESULTS/v5.jsonl" "$RESULTS/candidate.jsonl"

For screening, apply the archived patch to a fresh V5 checkout, build it, and set OVAL_DUEL_DISCOUNT=70, OVAL_ZONE_HOLD=3 or OVAL_ZONE_CREDIT=0.03 independently or in combination. All three screening switches default off. Use seeds 12000–12063. For the case-level regressions, mount placement_regressions.py, race.py, economy.py, evaluate.py and repair_eval.py under /bot, then run placement_regressions.py --kit /kit --output /results/metrics.json for each image.
